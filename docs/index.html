WHY DO VULNERABLE NEURONS FAIL AT DIFFERENT TIMES?

Stable does not always mean resilient.

A neuron may retain a stable homeostatic state yet become progressively slower to recover from perturbations. Add random fluctuations, and failure can become probabilistic. Explore this idea in a minimal two-variable model.

FROM MATHEMATICS TO BIOLOGY

What the model represents—and what it does not.

01 / MITOCHONDRIAL RESERVE

Energy and buffering capacity

M aggregates mitochondrial function, energetic reserve, and stress-buffering capacity. It is not a directly measured ATP concentration.

02 / COMPOSITE STRESS

Oxidative and damage burden

S represents a combined stress coordinate. Iron chemistry, lipid peroxidation and dopamine oxidation can motivate this coordinate but are not separate computed species.

03 / BIOLOGICAL CONTEXT

Neuromelanin is nuanced

Neuromelanin may sequester harmful compounds in health, while pathological changes can contribute to vulnerability. The present equations do not explicitly describe neuromelanin turnover.

04 / INTERPRETATION

Failure is operational

The modeled event is first entry into S > 3.5 and M < 0.15. This is not a mechanistic ferroptosis assay, diagnosis or survival-time prediction.

Background reading: Li et al., 2023 · Filimontseva et al., 2025 · Alves et al., 2026. These publications provide context, not extra simulated model mechanisms.

METHODS & TRANSPARENCY

A companion, not a prediction engine.

This educational site implements the deterministic drift and analytic equilibrium from the linked Python repository, together with RK4 deterministic integration and Euler–Maruyama stochastic integration. The manuscript uses SciPy RK45 for deterministic trajectories; therefore interactive deterministic curves may differ slightly from publication outputs.

The interactive Monte Carlo default uses fewer paths and a coarser timestep than the manuscript for responsiveness. Choosing the “manuscript resolution” preset restores N = 1,000, T = 500 and Δt = 0.02, and may take longer. Random seeds are reproducible within the browser implementation but do not produce identical paths to Python/NumPy. Always use the original repository for publication-grade reproduction.

λ is an effective, uncalibrated load, not a clinical exposure or elapsed disease time. Changing parameters beyond the reference values constitutes a hypothetical mathematical experiment. Positive survival equilibria exist only for λ below the displayed model-dependent boundary.
